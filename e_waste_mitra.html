📘 Learn the basics

Simple concepts everyone should know.

📱 What is e-waste?

Discarded electrical or electronic equipment, such as old phones, computers, chargers and appliances.

🌱 Why manage it carefully?

Some electronics contain materials that can harm people and the environment if dumped or handled carelessly.

🔧 Repair and reuse

If an item is safe and repairable, consider repairing it, using it again or donating through a trusted organisation.

♻️ Responsible recycling

Authorized recyclers can safely process equipment and recover useful materials while managing hazardous parts.

🔄 How recycling works

A simplified learning model, not a do-it-yourself instruction.

1. CollectKeep unwanted electronics separate from everyday rubbish.
2. SortTrained teams classify items by type.
3. ProcessAuthorized facilities safely separate components.
4. RecoverSuitable materials can be used again.

💡 Smart reuse ideas

📱 Old smartphone

If working, securely erase personal data before donating or passing it on.

💻 Old laptop

Ask a qualified technician about repair. Back up and securely erase data before transfer.

🔌 Charger and cable

Use only undamaged compatible chargers. Recycle unusable cables through an authorized channel.

🔋 Batteries

Never puncture, crush, burn or open batteries. Seek specialist collection advice for damaged batteries.

🎯 About this project

Aim: Spread awareness about electronic waste and encourage responsible repair, reuse and recycling.

Innovation: One mobile-friendly website combines an item guidance checker, learning concepts, recycling steps and an interactive quiz.

Limitations: This prototype gives general information. It does not identify items from photos, verify nearby collection centres or replace official local disposal instructions.

Safety: Never open, burn, crush or dismantle electronic devices or batteries. Use authorized collection channels and follow local rules. In India, check CPCB and local municipal guidance.
